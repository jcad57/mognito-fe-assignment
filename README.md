<h1>How to run</h1>

<h2>Physical Device:</h2>
<h3>Download Expo Go</h3>
<p>https://docs.expo.dev/get-started/set-up-your-environment/?platform=ios&device=physical</p>

<h3>Clone the repo to your IDE with GH CLI</h3>
<p><code>gh repo clone jcad57/mognito-fe-assignment</code></p>

<p>Or download files directly from GH repo and open in your IDE</p>

<h3>install dependencies</h3>
<p><code>npm i @supabase/supabase-js expo-router react-native-safe-area-context react-native-screens expo-linking expo-constants expo-status-bar </code></p>

<h3>Start the development server</h3>
<p><code>npx expo start</code></p>

<h3>Scan QR code on your phone and the app should run!</h3>
